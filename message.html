CLASSIFIED MESSAGE

For the person behind all the chaos.

Hey. I know you're hurting, and I know that hearing "move on" doesn't magically make anything easier. You don't have to wake up tomorrow and suddenly be completely okay. You don't have to pretend that you never cared, and you don't have to erase everything that mattered just because the chapter ended. Take your time. Have bad days. Have quiet days. Laugh at stupid things. Get annoyed over nonsense. Go outside. Eat something. Sleep properly. Do your work. Talk to the people who make life feel lighter. Slowly, without forcing it, start building your days again. There are still places you haven't seen, people you haven't met, things you haven't learned, ridiculous memories you haven't made and so many moments that haven't happened yet. One person being gone does not make the rest of your life empty. What happened mattered, but it does not get to decide what happens next. You are still allowed to become happy again, to become stronger, to laugh without guilt and to make a life that feels completely yours. You don't need to know exactly where you're going yet. Just keep moving toward the next good thing, however small it is.

There is still so much life waiting for you.
